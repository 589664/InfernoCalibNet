# Neural Networks and EfficientNet B3 Workflow

## Introduction to DCNN (Deep Convolutional Neural Network)

A **Deep Convolutional Neural Network (DCNN)** is a neural network architecture specifically designed for image-based tasks such as classification, detection, and segmentation. It typically consists of:

1. **Convolutional layers**: Apply filters to the input image to extract features.
2. **Pooling layers**: Reduce the dimensionality of feature maps.
3. **Fully connected layers**: Used for classification tasks.
4. **Activation functions**: Introduce non-linearity.
5. **Backpropagation**: Learn parameters by minimizing loss.

## EfficientNet B3 Overview

**EfficientNet** is a family of models that scale CNNs efficiently along depth, width, and resolution using a compound scaling method. EfficientNet B3 is larger than B0-B2 and smaller than B4-B7.

Key components:

- **Convolutional layers**
- **Batch Normalization** and **Swish activation**
- **Depthwise separable convolutions**
- **Global Average Pooling and Fully Connected layers**

## EfficientNet B3 as a DCNN

EfficientNet B3 retains all critical DCNN architectural elements, optimized for performance and efficiency.

## Workflow for EfficientNet Training with PyTorch

### 1. Dataset Preparation

The dataset is split into **training** and **validation** sets with multi-label annotations:

- `ImageID`, `Labels`, `Age`, `Gender`, `XrayView`, `MultiHotLabels`

### Helper Methods

- `load_image(image_path, img_size)`
- `compute_mean_std(image_paths, img_size)`

### 2. Dataset Class for DataLoader

The `XRayDataset` class handles:

- Loading and preprocessing images
- Fetching multi-hot encoded labels
- Applying transformations (resize, normalize)

### 3. Compute Mean and Standard Deviation

The dataset's **mean** and **standard deviation** are calculated using `compute_mean_std` and used for normalization.

### 4. DataLoader Setup

Handles:

- **Batching**
- **Shuffling** (training set only)

### 5. Verifying DataLoader

Check the shapes:

- **Images**: `(batch_size, channels, height, width)`
- **Labels**: `(batch_size, num_labels)`

Example Output:

- Images: `(16, 1, 1000, 1000)`
- Labels: `(16, 15)`

## Running Time Estimation for EfficientNet

### Key Parameters

- Model: EfficientNet B3
- Input size: 1000x1000 grayscale
- Dataset size: 150,000 images
- Batch size: 16
- GPU: NVIDIA RTX A4000 (16 GB)

### Time Estimations

#### Time Per Batch

~0.15 seconds

#### Number of Batches Per Epoch

$ \text{Batches per epoch} = \frac{150,000}{16} = 9,375 \text{ batches} $

#### Time Per Epoch

$ \text{Time per epoch} = 9,375 \times 0.15 \approx 1,406 \text{ seconds} \approx 23.4 \text{ minutes} $

#### Total Training Time

- 20 epochs: $ 23.4 \times 20 = 468 \text{ minutes} \approx 7.8 \text{ hours} $
- 10 epochs: $ 23.4 \times 10 = 234 \text{ minutes} \approx 3.9 \text{ hours} $

## Conclusion

EfficientNet B3 will efficiently train 150,000 grayscale images on the NVIDIA RTX A4000 GPU, completing 20 epochs in approximately **7.8 hours** or 10 epochs in **3.9 hours**.
